# Figure Inspiration References

- **Date:** 2026-06-26 21:22 +0200
- **Source:** Paper discussion

## Context
Earlier we logged that the current figures are not publishable quality
(see the script-isolation idea note). Before rebuilding each figure's
standalone script, we need a shared pool of high-quality, reusable
figure idioms from the Community Notes / polarization literature to
borrow design vocabulary from.

## Idea
Keep the five sources below as a figure-inspiration reference set. They
are NOT for citation in the manuscript prose — only for visual design
vocabulary when we redraw our figures. Each maps to a figure type we
need:

- arXiv:2409.10452 (Nakis et al., SGAAE, AISTATS 2025) — polytope
  projections, archetype membership plots, 2-level polarization
  diagrams. Borrow for: cluster / constituency geometry, polarization
  visualization.
- arXiv:2210.15723 (Wojcik et al., Birdwatch) — helpfulness-vs-polarity
  scatter, bridging acceptance curves, rating distributions. Borrow for:
  acceptance × helpfulness plots, classic CN bridging style.
- arXiv:2510.09585 (Mohammadi et al., four-year survey) — temporal
  evolution, taxonomy, participation/engagement distributions. Borrow
  for: survey-style overview, temporal trends.
- jonathanwarden.com/multidimensional-community-notes — polarity vs
  common-ground scatter (1D/2D/3D basis-change), synthetic-data polarity
  plot, 3D hemisphere. Borrow for: matrix-factorization geometry, the
  Conceptual Map figures.
- jonathanwarden.com/understanding-community-notes — ASCII regression-
  line diagrams, opinion-space projection scatter, Helpful/Unhelpful ×
  Left/Neutral/Right example-note tables. Borrow for: pedagogical
  regression-line figures, "common ground = intercept" exposition.

## Why It Matters
A consistent design vocabulary across the figure pool keeps the
manuscript visually coherent and lifts each redraw above a one-off
sketch. Drawing from peer-reviewed and well-regarded sources also makes
the visual choices defensible during review.
