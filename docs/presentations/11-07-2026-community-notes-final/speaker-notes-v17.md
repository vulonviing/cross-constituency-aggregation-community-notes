## 33. Gabriel Validation — 0:40

Validation is separate from aggregation. Stage 1 requires sourced factual
information with a visible external source pointer. Stage 2 scores the surviving
notes for rescue-worthiness. A coherent contextual claim can therefore stop at
Stage 1 when it lacks visible sourcing; that is a validation failure, not proof
that its claim is false.

## 35. What Makes a Note Worth Rescuing? — 0:35

Rescue-worthiness is a holistic zero-to-one-hundred judgment applied only after
Stage 1 identifies sourced factual information. The model evaluates visible
source specificity, claim–source connection, self-contained explanation,
neutral language, and concise constructive presentation. It does not see the
original post, open the URL, or independently verify the source contents.
