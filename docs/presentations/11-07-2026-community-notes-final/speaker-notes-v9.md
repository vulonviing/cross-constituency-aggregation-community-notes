## 30. Gabriel Validation — 0:40

Validation is deliberately separate from aggregation. Stage 1 classifies the
note’s nature and requires a visible external source pointer. Only sourced
factual information continues. Stage 2 scores those notes from zero to one
hundred for source traceability, connection to the claim, clarity, neutrality,
and constructive presentation. Fifty is the historical working threshold. A
coherent or plausible contextual claim can therefore stop at Stage 1 when it
lacks visible sourcing. That is a validation failure, not proof that its claim
is false.

## 31. From Candidates to Validated Rescues — 0:35

Under the historical GPT-4o-mini baseline, 8,051 of the 13,655 candidates pass
Stage 1, and 3,896 also score at least fifty in Stage 2. Among the Stage 1 stops,
474 were classified as unsourced context or claims. These are historical,
model-dependent counts. A MiMo v2.5 Pro rerun is underway with a higher
reasoning budget, and we expect more consistent classification. We do not yet
claim that more notes will pass; final counts will change when the rerun is
complete.
