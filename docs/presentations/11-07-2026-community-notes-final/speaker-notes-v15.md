## 35. What Makes a Note Worth Rescuing? — 0:35

Rescue-worthiness is a holistic zero-to-one-hundred judgment applied only after
Stage 1 identifies sourced factual information. The model looks for a specific,
traceable source pointer; a clear connection between that pointer and the
factual claim; a self-contained explanation; neutral language; and concise,
constructive presentation. The historical display threshold is fifty. A key
limitation is that the judge sees only the note text. It does not see the
original post, open the URL, or verify the source’s contents. The score therefore
measures visible note quality, not factual truth established through browsing.
